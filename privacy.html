Privacy Policy

Wild Goose Chase · Snappy Fox Ltd

The short version: Wild Goose Chase is an anonymous game. It does not require an account, ask for a name or email address, sell personal information, or use advertising analytics.

Who is responsible

Snappy Fox Ltd operates Wild Goose Chase. For privacy questions, contact hello@snappyfox.co.uk.

What the game stores on your device

Wild Goose Chase stores game settings and progress in your browser's local storage, such as your sound setting, personal best, run count and purchased upgrades. This information stays in that browser unless you clear browser data. It is not sent to our game server by the game.

What our service processes

When your browser loads the game, our hosting providers may process limited technical request information, such as IP address, browser or device information, requested URL, date and time, and error or security logs. We use this information only to deliver, secure, maintain, and troubleshoot the service.

Cookies, sharing, and advertising

Wild Goose Chase does not set advertising or analytics cookies and does not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising. When the game runs on a third-party games portal, that portal may serve advertising and set its own cookies under its own privacy policy.

Service providers

We use hosting and infrastructure providers to make Wild Goose Chase available. Those providers may process technical information on our behalf to operate and secure their services.

Retention

Device-local game data remains until you clear it. Operational logs are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for security, troubleshooting, and service operation, subject to our providers' retention practices.

Your choices and rights

You can clear local game data through your browser settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask about personal information we hold about you. Contact us at the address above and we will respond as required by applicable law.

Children

Wild Goose Chase is a general-audience arcade game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy when the service or applicable law changes. We will post the updated version here and change its effective date.
